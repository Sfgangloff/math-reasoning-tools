# Meta-lists and Surveys

Curated collections of MCP servers and academic surveys on LLM-based theorem proving.

| Resource | URL | Notes |
|----------|-----|-------|
| awesome-mcp-servers (wong2) | [wong2/awesome-mcp-servers](https://github.com/wong2/awesome-mcp-servers) | Most widely cited curated list. Good breadth. |

## Academic Surveys

| Resource | URL | Notes |
|----------|-----|-------|
| DL4TP | [zhaoyu-li/DL4TP](https://github.com/zhaoyu-li/DL4TP) | COLM 2024 survey on deep learning for theorem proving. Covers Lean4, Coq, Isabelle. Best single overview of the field. |
| awesome-formal-verification | [ElNiak/awesome-formal-verification](https://github.com/ElNiak/awesome-formal-verification) | Broader formal methods. Includes model checking, static analysis, not just theorem provers. |
